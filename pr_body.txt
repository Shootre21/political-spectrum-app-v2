💡 What: Replaced inline RegExp instantiations with `indexOf` loops and used early-exit loops instead of `.filter()`.
🎯 Why: String matching operations in hot paths cause memory allocations and unnecessary processing.
📊 Impact: Over 2x performance improvement in `calculateFramingScore` and significant reductions in other string matching functions.
🔬 Measurement: Verified with custom benchmarking scripts showing improvements from ~1000ms down to ~400ms for 10k iterations of `calculateFramingScore`.
