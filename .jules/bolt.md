## 2024-06-05 - Lexicon Search Optimization
**Learning:** In hot loops checking for occurrences of multiple terms against a string, dynamically instantiating `new RegExp(term, 'gi')` and using `match` is extremely slow compared to simple string primitives, especially if the terms do not contain complex regex patterns. In our lexicon-based bias detection, replacing inline global RegExp instantiations with `indexOf` loops resulted in a ~4x performance improvement during benchmarking without altering string-matching behavior.
**Action:** When implementing simple exact-substring counting within loops, explicitly prefer optimized `indexOf` while-loops over dynamic `RegExp` unless advanced pattern matching is strictly required. Ensure `if (term.length === 0) continue;` is present to prevent infinite loops.
