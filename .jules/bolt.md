## 2025-02-28 - Regex instantiation in hot loops
**Learning:** Instantiating `new RegExp()` inside hot loops (like text scoring for framing or emotion based on large lexicons) is a major performance bottleneck due to dynamic regex compilation. Additionally, passing raw strings into `new RegExp` can cause `SyntaxError`s or unexpected behavior if the strings contain special characters (like `+` or `*`).
**Action:** Replace inline `new RegExp(..., 'gi')` calls and `.match()` inside hot string-searching loops with custom `indexOf` while-loops. Ensure to handle the edge case of empty strings (`if (term.length === 0) continue;`) to prevent infinite loops, and advance the search index by the length of the matched term (`pos += term.length`) to replicate non-overlapping match behavior safely.
