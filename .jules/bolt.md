## 2024-06-03 - Optimize Lexicon Analysis with Module-Scoped Caches and `indexOf`
**Learning:** In text analysis hot paths within Node/Bun (such as those iterating over large lexicons like `TOPIC_LEXICONS`, `EMOTIONAL_LEXICON`, and `SOURCE_TYPE_INDICATORS`), dynamically instantiating `new RegExp(term, 'gi')` and relying on `.toLowerCase()` or `array.filter(...).length > 0` incurs significant CPU and GC overhead. Even though V8 regex engines are fast, constructing new regex objects repeatedly in loops is costly.
**Action:** Always pre-process and cache static analysis terms at the module level (e.g., pre-lowercasing them). Replace dynamically constructed global/case-insensitive RegExp searches with custom `indexOf` while-loops inside standard `for` loops for large text processing pipelines to drastically improve throughput.
