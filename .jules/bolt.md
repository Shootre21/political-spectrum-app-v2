## 2024-06-19 - Fast Literal Substring Counting
**Learning:** Instantiating `new RegExp(term, 'gi')` inside hot loops for every term across multiple lexicons is incredibly slow in Node.js/Bun. However, completely pre-caching global regex instances at the module scope surprisingly also performed poorly for arrays of terms due to the overhead of the `.match()` internally allocating new arrays of matches. The most performant method by far (~2-3x faster) for counting literal non-overlapping string occurrences (case-insensitive) is to manually implement an `indexOf` while-loop against a pre-lowercased target text and a pre-lowercased search term (`while ((pos = lowerText.indexOf(search, pos)) !== -1) { count++; pos += search.length; }`).
**Action:** When performing heavy text analysis, especially dictionary matching against multiple terms, favor manual `indexOf` loops over inline regular expressions or even pre-compiled regular expressions if extracting matches is not necessary and only counting/existence is required.
